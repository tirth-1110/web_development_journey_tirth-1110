b tag is used to make text bold

i tag is used to make text italic

u tag is used to underline any word or sentence in a paragraph

s tag is used to make text strikethrough

del tag is also used to make text strikethrough

sub tag is used to make text subscript (below the line)

sup tag is used to make text superscript (above the line)

you can combine tags to create complex formatting effects such as bold and italic or bold and underline or italic and underline or bold, italic and underline
