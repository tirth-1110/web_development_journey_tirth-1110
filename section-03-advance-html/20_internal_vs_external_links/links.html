Internal and External Links

In web development, links are used to navigate between different pages or resources. There are two main types of links: internal and external.

Internal Links

Internal links point to other pages within the same website. Internal links are useful for navigating within a website and improving user experience.

External Links

External links point to pages on other websites. External links are useful for providing additional information and resources to users.
